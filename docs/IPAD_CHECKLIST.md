# iPad checklist (Phase 6, run by Nick)

Run on the oldest and the newest iPad you have. Tick each item; note the iPad model and iOS
version at the top of each run.

## Install and launch

- [ ] Opens in Safari at the Pages URL.
- [ ] Share > Add to Home Screen shows the ball icon and the name "Tumble Trail".
- [ ] Launching from the home screen is full screen (no Safari bars).
- [ ] Offline launch works after one online run (turn on Airplane Mode, kill the app, relaunch).

## Performance and feel

- [ ] FPS readout (parent menu) is at least 55 on the oldest iPad.
- [ ] No pinch-zoom, text loupe, text selection or rubber-banding anywhere.
- [ ] Audio plays after the first tap; hardware volume buttons change the level.
- [ ] Rotating the iPad in the middle of a level is fine (camera and controls recover).
- [ ] Backgrounding the app and returning does not leave the ball driving.

## Parent controls

- [ ] The parent gate (hold top-left and bottom-right corners for 3 s) cannot be triggered with
      one hand.
- [ ] The gate works from every screen, including during play.
- [ ] Guided Access steps on the parent card match what the iPad shows.

## Child protocol

- [ ] Three separate sessions with the 3-year-old. The adult stays silent and does not touch the
      iPad. The child starts from the title and reaches the finish of level 1 within 3 minutes,
      and asks to play again.
- [ ] One Big-kid session with a 5-8-year-old, recording the number of falls per level.
